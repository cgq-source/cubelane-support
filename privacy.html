Privacy policy

Cube Arrow 3D privacy policy · Updated 4 October 2026. Developer: Guiquan Chen. Your board, coins, settings and reward identifiers are stored on your device. There is no separate Cube Arrow 3D account or developer-operated game server. Optional iCloud progress sync uses Apple’s private CloudKit database for your completed level IDs and used free hints. It does not synchronize coins or the current board. Sync is on by default where configured; you can turn it off. Turning it off does not delete an existing cloud copy. Local backups may be kept to protect progress during a merge. Device removal or iCloud data management can remove stored data; use Apple’s iCloud controls for cloud data.

Optional Game Center rankings are managed by Apple. After you choose to sign in, the app submits your highest cleared level; your Game Center nickname, rank and score are publicly visible. Practice and test saves never submit scores. Game Center availability depends on your Apple account and network. Manage your Game Center profile and privacy in Apple Settings. Optional rewarded ads use Google Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform. When an ad or consent request is made, Google may process IP address, approximate location, device identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostics to deliver ads, prevent fraud and handle consent. Availability and consent choices depend on your region. Ad privacy choices appear in Settings when required. This app does not request tracking permission or access IDFA without authorization. This version configures optional AdMob rewarded ads. Ad personalization and publisher first-party ID are disabled, and the maximum ad content rating is General. Non-personalized ads still process the data described above; this does not mean no data collection. UMP checks consent before ad requests. Consent can be managed in Settings when required; refusing or withdrawing consent does not prevent normal play. Availability depends on consent, inventory, network and provider approval. Closing early or an unavailable ad gives no reward. Only a confirmed earned callback grants 60 local coins once, with an atomic local save; coins cannot be purchased, transferred or redeemed for money. Store links, update checks and iCloud communicate with Apple; the public support site is hosted by GitHub. Those providers process network data under their own policies. Contact us about privacy or data rights; do not send passwords or payment details.
