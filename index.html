Support & contact

Cube Arrow 3D has 300 spatial candy puzzles. Progress through levels in order; there is no level picker. First-time players complete a hands-on tutorial; replay it from Settings and skip the replay if desired. The global leaderboard ranks the highest level cleared. Nine languages, separate music/effect/haptic switches and optional iCloud progress sync are supported. Coins and the current board stay on this device. For help, send the version, device and reproduction steps to abc466254511@gmail.com.

Tap a candy to slide it along an unobstructed arrow path. Drag a candy to turn a layer; drag empty space to look around; pinch to zoom. The first undo per level is free, then 10 coins. Hints cost 30 coins; the first three levels each include one free hint. Buy one extra layer turn for 20 coins, up to three purchases per level. Only unused purchased turns carry forward. No purchases after clearing a level. First clears give 20 coins, or 40 total every tenth level; replaying gives no reward. An optional completed rewarded ad gives 60 coins only upon provider confirmation. Hints smoothly turn toward the arrow, highlight the target and perform the move automatically. A layer-turn hint also extracts one movable candy when available, for one hint charge. Only tapping a blocked candy loses a heart. With N candies at the start, the level ends on floor(N/2)+1 blocked taps; removing candies and undoing do not restore hearts. After failure, retry is the only option; ads cannot revive the level. The four-step first-time tutorial finishes only after you clear its practice board.
